What we clean

Every shared space, every visit.

A written checklist for your building, agreed at the site visit. This is what a standard visit covers.

Stairwells

Every visit includes

  • Sweep and mop hard stairs, or vacuum carpeted flights
  • Wipe handrails, banisters and spindles
  • Remove cobwebs from corners and ceilings within reach
  • Pick up litter from steps and under the stairs

Hallways & entrances

Every visit includes

  • Clean entrance-door glass inside and out
  • Wipe the intercom panel, door handles and push plates
  • Wipe letterbox fronts and put junk mail neatly to one side
  • Shake or vacuum the mat and sweep the step

Landings

Every visit includes

  • Vacuum or mop every landing
  • Wipe skirting, sills and ledges within reach
  • Clean light switches and fire-door glass
  • Check escape routes are clear, and report it if not

Lifts

Every visit includes

  • Clean the lift floor
  • Wipe walls and mirror, and polish steel panels
  • Clean the buttons inside and on each floor
  • Clear grit from the door tracks

Extras we can quote for

  • Carpet deep clean
  • High-level dusting
  • Bin store clean
  • Changing communal light bulbs (bulbs supplied by you)
  • One-off deep cleans for handovers and move-ins

Not included

  • Inside individual flats
  • External or high-level windows
  • Work needing ladders above two metres
  • Removing bulky waste (we report it so it can be collected)

In freezing or very wet weather we may dry-mop instead of wet-mop, to keep floors safe underfoot.

Visit records

How you’ll know we’ve been.

After every visit you get a short report by email: the date, time in and time out, what was cleaned, photos of the entrance, stairs, landings and lift, and anything that needs attention. We also sign a visit sheet on the noticeboard, so residents can see when we’ve been.

What we report

  • Blown bulbs or emergency lights that look off
  • Fire doors propped open or not closing
  • Bikes, prams or rubbish blocking stairs and escape routes
  • Damage, graffiti, leaks or damp patches
  • Signs of pests
  • Fly-tipping or bulky waste

We report what we see so you can send the right person. We don’t carry out fire risk assessments, repairs or inspections. Needles, broken glass or bodily fluids: we don’t handle these, and we tell you straight away.

Keys, fobs and access
